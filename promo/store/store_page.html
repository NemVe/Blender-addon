How it works

Two poses and one button

1

Pose it, record it

Select the parts and press New Move: where they stand is Before. Move them and press Record on After.

2

Build: one slider

Build Sliders makes the bones, binds the parts and puts the move on one slider, in the panel and as a handle in the viewport.

3

As many parts as you like

Every selected part shares the move, and each one can wait its turn.

Twelve tools

For the moves machines make

01 · Timing

Give every part its own delay

A Delay says how long a part waits before it starts. Parts leave in order and overlap, so a machine reads as built instead of as one rubber object.

02 · Spread

Spread the timing into a wave

One dialog fills every Delay, or every copy's Lead: round a ring, down a chain, along an axis, by distance or at random.

03 · Pivot

Turn around any point you want

Each part turns about its origin, the 3D cursor or its base. The poses stay as recorded; the way between them swings about the pivot.

04 · Steps

Add steps in between

Pose the middle of the move as well. Each stretch between two poses gets its own speed.

05 · Curves

Curve the path, then edit the curve

A part follows a curve you bend in edit mode. Its two ends always stay on Before and After.

06 · Speed

Smooth, or perfectly even

Even speed, Smooth, Slow start or Slow stop, for each move.

07 · Chain

Chain parts, so the joints follow

Make Chain hangs each segment from the one before it in one click: a finger, a leaf, a crane arm.

08 · Curl

Curl a whole chain at once

Bend the first joint, and Curl repeats the bend down the chain.

09 · Ring

Ring one part into many

Copies round a centre ride along with the part, each moving from its own place. Fill the gaps keeps the copies you already modelled.

10 · Mirror

Mirror the copies

A copy can mirror its leader's move across its own X, Y or Z: the leader's left becomes the copy's right.

11 · Combine

Combine moves under one control

Play several moves one after another from a single slider, or overlap them with a delay.

12 · Animate

And animate it all in one click

Set the frames and press Animate: the slider is keyed from the current frame. Or drag the handle in the viewport.

Built for real files

It keeps working after the demo

Plays without the add-on

The rig is plain bones, constraints and simple drivers. Files open, play and render anywhere, even with Auto Run Python Scripts off.

Copies keep working

Alt+D a whole rigged model: the copy gets its own slider and its own animation.

Edit after Build

Rename or delete a part, change a delay, or press Pause and pose again: the next Build takes it in.

Look before you build

The eye shows Before or After. Pause lets you pose freely, and To Before puts everything back.

It tells you what happened

The report box says what each button did and what to do next, and turns red for a warning.

Bones too

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to know how to rig?

No. Select the parts, pose them, press Build.

Does the finished file need CopyThat?

No. It plays and renders without it.

Can I change the move after Build?

Yes. Press Pause and pose freely, or record a pose again, then Build.

Is it for characters?

It records the poses of any bones. It is made for machines and objects that move as solid parts. For a character's skin and face, use a character rigging tool.

Where is the full guide?

In the Documentation tab: every button, every dialog, and the flower step by step.
