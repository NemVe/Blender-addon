Voice and sound of promo v2.2: where each comes from, and under what licence.

Voiceover (open_a.wav ... end.wav, vo.json)
  Spoken by Chatterbox (Resemble AI, MIT, https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox,
  chatterbox-tts 0.1.7, the ResembleAI/chatterbox weights, MIT) with its default
  voice, on the GPU, in a few continuous passages (vo_script.py), exaggeration 0.65,
  cfg 0.4, temperature 0.8. Its output carries Resemble's Perth watermark, which is
  inaudible. The passages are cut into lines at the word times (cut_vo.py).
  The audio is generated: no person's recording is in it, and it needs no credit.
  The take of each passage and the word times were chosen and measured with
  faster-whisper (MIT) and the OpenAI Whisper small.en model (MIT).
  v2.1's voice was Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) through kokoro-onnx (MIT); make_vo.py is
  kept as the fallback and for the audio helpers cut_vo.py uses.

Sound effects (click*.wav, whoosh.wav, pop*.wav, tick.wav, sfx.json)
  Made by make_sfx.py from numpy noise and sine waves. Nothing is sampled from
  a recording or a sound pack, so they are CC0 1.0 (public domain): use them
  anywhere, no credit needed.

Rebuild:  <python 3.11 venv with chatterbox-tts> gen_vo.py --out <takes folder>
          python cut_vo.py --takes <takes folder> --cache <folder for the Whisper model>
          python make_sfx.py
          python ../make_timeline.py
