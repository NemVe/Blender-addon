Voice and sound of promo v2.1: where each comes from, and under what licence.

Voiceover (hook1.wav ... end.wav, vo.json)
  Spoken by Kokoro-82M v1.0, voice am_michael: model and voice packs are
  Apache-2.0 (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M). It ran on the CPU
  through kokoro-onnx (MIT, https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) with
  the ONNX model and voices of that project's model-files-v1.0 release.
  espeak-ng (GPL-3.0, inside espeakng-loader) turns the text into phonemes; it
  is a tool here, and the audio is not a derivative of it.
  The audio is generated: no person's recording is in it, and it needs no credit.
  The word times in vo.json were measured with faster-whisper (MIT) and the
  OpenAI Whisper base.en model (MIT); see align_vo.py.

Sound effects (click*.wav, whoosh.wav, pop*.wav, tick.wav, sfx.json)
  Made by make_sfx.py from numpy noise and sine waves. Nothing is sampled from
  a recording or a sound pack, so they are CC0 1.0 (public domain): use them
  anywhere, no credit needed.

Rebuild:  python make_vo.py --models <folder with kokoro-v1.0.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin>
          python align_vo.py
          python make_sfx.py
          python ../make_timeline.py
